Parent Manager · Direction B

Clear Utility

Fast, precise and confident, like a pro tool that gets out of the way.

1. Mood and principles

  • Information density and speed first: crisp white, cool greys and one strong blue.
  • Flat surfaces, edge-to-edge grouped lists and swipe-friendly rows, like a native reminders app.
  • Feels efficient and trustworthy; the risk is that it feels like work at 3 a.m.

6. Why this works for a tired parent

One-handed reach

The quick-add field sits right above the keyboard and the actions sit in a bottom toolbar, but week navigation and Export are at the top, which is a stretch on a big phone.

Readable in low light

High contrast is very legible, but bright white backgrounds and dense rows can glare at night. Dark mode is crisp, though cold.

Calm, not busy

Dense by design: the whole week and most of the list fit on one screen with no scrolling. That is fast, but it can feel like work at 3 a.m.

Contrast

The strongest of the three: body text is 17.9:1 in light mode and 15.9:1 in dark mode. Every text pair passes WCAG AA.

Static design exploration, not the app. Colors, type and layouts are proposals for review. Fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back to system fonts offline.
