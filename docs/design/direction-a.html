Parent Manager · Direction A

Calm Kitchen

Warm, quiet and reassuring, like a well-kept kitchen notebook.

1. Mood and principles

  • Calm, not clinical: warm paper tones, a sage green primary and one restrained terracotta accent.
  • Screen titles in a soft serif for warmth; everything else in the system font so dynamic type stays native.
  • Lowers the stress of an overloaded day rather than adding energy. Recommended direction.

6. Why this works for a tired parent

One-handed reach

The single primary action on each screen ("Add a meal", the add-item bar, "Add oat milk") sits in the bottom third. Rows are 56 pt tall and the whole row toggles the checkbox.

Readable in low light

A warm off-white instead of pure white cuts glare, and dark mode uses a warm charcoal, never pure black or blue light. Body text stays at 17 pt in the system font.

Calm, not busy

One accent color, few borders and no badges. "Tonight" leads the planner, so the one decision that matters right now is the first thing a tired parent sees.

Contrast

Body text is 13.4:1 in light mode and 14.9:1 in dark mode. Every text pair passes WCAG AA; the terracotta accent is kept to fills and large text in light mode (4.2:1).

Static design exploration, not the app. Colors, type and layouts are proposals for review. Fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back to system fonts offline.
