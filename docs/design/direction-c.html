Village

Friendly, warm and optimistic, built around the village that helps the parents.

1. Mood and principles

  • Coral and sunshine on a warm cream, with big rounded shapes and member avatars everywhere.
  • Celebrates small wins and makes sharing visible: you can see who added and who bought what.
  • The most personality, and the most work: illustrations and motion need care.

6. Why this works for a tired parent

One-handed reach

Big 48 to 64 pt targets, a floating add button and a one-tap grid of usual items make capture a single thumb tap. The floating tab bar keeps all four tabs in thumb reach.

Readable in low light

The cream background is gentle, but the coral and yellow are the brightest colors of the three. Dark mode needs extra care so the accents don't glow.

Calm, not busy

The busiest direction: avatars, blobs and celebrations. Warm and motivating for some parents, noisy for others; motion must respect Reduce Motion.

Contrast

Body text is 14.1:1 in light mode and 15.3:1 in dark mode. Every text pair passes WCAG AA; the yellow accent is never used for text (1.5:1), only as a fill under dark text (9.1:1).

Static design exploration, not the app. Colors, type and layouts are proposals for review. Fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back to system fonts offline.
